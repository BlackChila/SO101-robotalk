# RoboTalk code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to discuss, use and contribute to RoboTalk. This policy
applies to this repository's issues, pull requests and other project discussions.

## Participation

- Treat people respectfully, regardless of background, identity or experience.
- Explain disagreements and technical concerns constructively. Critique the work,
  and give people room to learn or correct mistakes.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and respect contributors' time.
- Ask permission before sharing someone else's identifying information,
  conversations, recordings or images.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual misconduct, personal attacks,
deliberate disclosure of private information, spam and repeated disruption are
not acceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

For a non-sensitive moderation request, mention
[@BlackChila](https://github.com/BlackChila) in the relevant discussion and keep
the request brief. Do not repeat private information or escalate an argument.

For harassment, threats, exposed personal information or other sensitive abuse,
use GitHub's **Report content / Report abuse** action where available, or
[contact GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
These reports go through GitHub; they are not a private inbox for the maintainer.
See [GitHub's reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

The maintainer may ask participants to change their behavior, hide or remove
disruptive content, close or lock discussions, and restrict participation for
serious or repeated violations. Moderation will consider context and focus on
keeping participation constructive. Reporter privacy will be respected when
handling information available to the maintainer.

Security vulnerabilities belong in the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).
